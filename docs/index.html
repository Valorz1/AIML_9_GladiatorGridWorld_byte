Gladiator Grid World

A Q-Learning agent starts with no idea where anything is. Over 5,000 attempts it learns to cross the arena to the gold goal, avoid every trap, and take the shortest possible route. Move the slider to see how its best route changed during training.

Arena rules

Each move−1
Walk into a wall or the edge−5, stay in place
Step on a trap−100, episode lost
Reach the goal+100, episode won
Still walking after 100 movesEpisode lost

Because every move costs a point, the agent earns the most by reaching the goal in the fewest steps. With "Show what it learned" on, each tile is shaded by how valuable the agent thinks it is (brighter is better) and the arrow is the move it would make there. Tiles far from the winning route stay dark, because the agent rarely needed to visit them.
